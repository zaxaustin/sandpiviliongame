# Mission System Plan — The Sand Pavilion

**Status:** Draft for implementation
**Date:** 2026-08-07 (ranks ordered easiest → hardest: F → E → D → C → B → A → S)
**Author:** the steward
**Filed:** 2026-08-07. See `plans/done/NEW-PLANS-RECONCILED-2026-08-07.md` for what
this already has in the tree and what it would cost.

**Purpose:** Give people (and the steward) real foundations, then a clear path
into work that is larger than any single life. The sky is the limit; the system
shows there is a way to tackle seemingly hopeless problems without requiring
anyone to act alone forever.

This is not a traditional quest system. There are no XP, leaderboards, or
personal power scores. Motivation comes from earned ability, visible
contribution, and the quiet knowledge that the work can outlive you.

---

## Core Philosophy

The Pavilion follows a simple progressive logic drawn from the Buddhist path the
steward is walking:

**Build yourself up before you can reliably help others.**

- First cultivate a stable personal foundation aligned with the Eightfold Path
  (Right View, Intention, Speech, Action, Livelihood, Effort, Mindfulness,
  Concentration).
- Only then move into wider circles of helpful action.
- The project itself is intended as a living example of how far one person can
  come in their own practice, and as a place where others can find guidance for
  a proper, civilized way of being — one that appreciates the teachings that
  brought us here.

Additional standing principles:

- The Pavilion does not control direction. It supplies foundations, workbenches,
  and ethical orientation.
- Library and source texts (including the Eightfold Path materials already
  present) remain fully open at every stage.
- Higher Missions become fully usable only after a person has demonstrated they
  can stand on their own feet and transmit learning.
- Progress is measured by artifacts and hosted work, never by time spent or
  claims.
- Everything stays local-first. Sharing happens through packets, bequests, and
  optional Commons.
- Alignment with the Eightfold Path is a hard filter for any Mission that claims
  to serve humanity.

## Rank Framework

Ranks communicate scale, difficulty, time horizon, and required collaboration.
They are labels of seriousness, not a personal power ladder.

**Progression from easiest to hardest:**

| Rank | Scope | Time Horizon | Collaboration | Knowledge State | Example |
|------|-------|--------------|---------------|-----------------|---------|
| **F** | Personal foundation | Months–few years | Self + optional mentors | Known skills applied with integrity and aligned with the Eightfold Path | Right Livelihood + graduate at least one independent course you designed, completed, and made ready for others |
| **E** | Household / very local project | Weeks–1 year | Friends, family, or 1–2 helpers | Mostly existing knowledge; consistency is the hard part | Build and keep a community well working, open and run a small helpful shop or tool library, start a reliable shared composting / food loop |
| **D** | Neighborhood / small local system | 6 months–3 years | A few neighbors + light management / operations skill | Known techniques + basic coordination and business sense | Design and run a neighborhood permaculture / ecosystem-balancing project that measurably helps soil, water, food or habitat and can continue if the original person steps back |
| **C** | Regional / systemic redesign | 1–10 years | Small coordinated groups | Mostly known techniques, hard execution | Open, resilient permaculture + supply chain for a bioregion |
| **B** | Continental / multi-system | 5–25 years | Large open collaboration | Frontier but plausible | Deployable free-energy or ultra-high-efficiency energy system (open hardware + open science) |
| **A** | Global / civilizational | 10–50 years | Massive multi-disciplinary coordination | Requires breakthroughs + redesign of incentives | Structural end to chronic world hunger, or a global open knowledge + manufacturing commons |
| **S** | Multi-planetary / multi-generational | 50–150+ years | Civilizational + multi-generational | Deep unknowns | Self-sustaining human presence on Ganymede (or equivalent outer-solar-system body) |

**Order:** F → E → D → C → B → A → S (easiest to hardest)

**Why this progression**
F is the starting personal foundation — Right Livelihood lived in alignment with
the Eightfold Path, plus the ability to design, complete, and transmit at least
one independent course. This is the deliberate self-cultivation step.
From there the scale and difficulty increase: E (household / close circle), D
(neighborhood coordination), then regional (C), continental (B), global (A), and
finally multi-generational / multi-planetary (S).

This mirrors both common light-novel / ranking systems (F as the entry rank, S
as the peak) and the steward's practice: build a solid personal foundation
first, then take on wider circles of care.

F-rank remains the formal personal gate. Higher-rank contribution tools open
more fully after F recognition. E and D work can happen alongside or after the
journey toward F.

### Nesting Rule

Every higher-rank Mission must expose lower-rank work packages that a single
person can actually finish.
No Mission may exist only as a grand statement.
"What's missing?" is a permanent, first-class section in every Mission Workspace
so anyone can propose or fill gaps.

## F-Rank: The Personal Threshold

**Name:** Establish a Right Livelihood and Graduate an Independent Path

**Requirements (both must be true):**

1. **Right Livelihood**
   A form of livelihood that is self-sustaining, does not require deception,
   exploitation, or the destruction of others' conditions for living well, and
   leaves surplus attention and energy for contribution. It is lived in
   conscious alignment with the Eightfold Path.

2. **Independent Course Graduation**
   The person has designed, completed, and made publish-ready at least one
   independent course using the Pavilion's own tools (see
   `INDEPENDENT-COURSE-GRADUATION.md`).

Recognition is local and honest. The record lives in the person's data. Higher
Mission surfaces become more fully available once the flag is present. There is
no public badge or ranking of people.

---

## E-Rank and D-Rank in Practice

**E-Rank** is the first step outward from the personal foundation.
Plenty of knowledge already exists; the Mission is the doing, the coordination
of a few close people (friends/family), and the persistence to keep something
useful running. Courses on basic operations, simple bookkeeping, facilitation,
and practical skills are the natural supports.

**D-Rank** is the next step into coordinating neighbors and carrying light
managerial / business responsibility for a local ecological or community good. A
solid F-rank foundation makes success far more likely. Independent courses on
soil, water, facilitation, light enterprise, etc., feed directly into these
Missions.

Both ranks keep the same open contribution style: permanent "What's missing?"
sections, hosted packets, and the ability for any individual to leave a real
piece.

---

## Motivation Without Manipulation

- After F-rank the Mission Board becomes more alive and useful.
- Quiet collective signals only: number of active carriers, recent hosted work,
  open work packages, last bequest left.
- No personal scores, streaks, or leaderboards.
- The emotional core is: "I am no longer only consuming. I can now leave
  something the next person can stand on."
- Language normalises "I carried this for a season and left it better."
- The Pavilion itself is offered as a living example of long practice and as a
  source of guidance for a civilized way of being that remembers the teachings
  that brought us here.

## Starter Content Recommendation

- Seed a few clear E-rank and D-rank Missions so early practical action has a
  home.
- Seed 2–3 well-written C- or B-rank Missions that already contain concrete
  first work packages.
- Place the Ganymede S-rank Mission (with early funding / feasibility notes) as
  the long horizon.
- Early independent courses published by graduates should be allowed (and
  encouraged) to become living contributions to any rank of Mission.
- "Propose a new Mission" is itself an F-rank-or-higher action; E and D
  proposals can be lighter.

## Implementation Notes

- Mission data lives alongside existing curriculum / investigation data.
- Each Mission opens into a dedicated Mission Workspace (see
  `MISSION-WORKSPACE-PLAN.md`).
- Graduation flag is a simple local derived check or explicit record.
- UI uses the large, calm work-surface pattern already planned for the Learning
  Tree so dense information stays readable.
- The Eightfold Path materials already present in the Library and Keep remain
  the living ethical reference.

---

## Success Criteria for the System Itself

- A person can move from first opening the Pavilion through E-rank practical
  action and into F-rank recognition without being told what to want.
- After F-rank they can immediately find concrete, doable work packages on D, C
  and higher Missions.
- Work left in a Mission can be picked up by someone years later with clear
  provenance and open questions.
- The system never requires a central server or account to function for a single
  user.
- Reputation, when it appears, comes only from inspectable hosted work.
- The Pavilion itself functions as a quiet example of long personal practice and
  as a place of guidance for civilized being.

This plan keeps the sky visible while giving people a real floor to stand on —
first under their own feet, then under the feet of those around them, and
eventually under the longer work of humanity.
The next detailed document is the Mission Workspace itself.

## Editorial note — the earlier draft

A first version of this plan was written the same day with **five** ranks
(F, C, B, A, S) and no Buddhist framing in the Core Philosophy. It is superseded
by this one, which adds **E** and **D** — the household and neighborhood
scales — and states the self-cultivation logic outright. The gap the earlier
draft left was real and worth naming: it jumped from "get your own life in
order" straight to "redesign a bioregion", with nothing in between that a person
could actually start on a Saturday.

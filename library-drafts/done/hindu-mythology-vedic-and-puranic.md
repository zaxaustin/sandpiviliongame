# Hindu Mythology, Vedic and Puranic

**Tradition:** Hindu
**License:** Public Domain (pre-1923 publication)
**Source URL:** https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.47770
**Attribution:** W. J. Wilkins (1900, 2nd edition)

## Summary

A comprehensive 1900 sourcebook working systematically through Vedic and Puranic mythology — the actual stories behind the gods named elsewhere on this shelf, not just their philosophy. Real chapters on Shiva and Ganesha specifically, drawn from the Puranas and epics (Ramayana, Mahabharata) with sources cited throughout, not retold from memory.

## Section 1 — Why this belongs beside the Gita and the Upanishads

Those two shelved texts are ethics and metaphysics; this is the narrative layer underneath both — who these figures actually are, their relationships, their stories — the same way Wilkins' contemporaries used mythology to make the more abstract texts legible. Ganesha and Shiva specifically get real, sourced treatment here, not a passing mention.

## Section 2 — A real scholarly apparatus, not a retelling

Wilkins cites his sources directly (the Vishnu Purana, the Ramayana, specific chapter numbers) throughout rather than presenting myth as his own narrative — closer to a careful compilation than a storybook. Worth reading as the reference this Library reaches for when a story needs its actual source traced, the same role Bulfinch or the Great Indian Epics retelling play for their own traditions.

# The Serpent Power

**Tradition:** Tantra
**License:** Public Domain (pre-1923 publication)
**Source URL:** https://archive.org/details/dli.ernet.28781
**Attribution:** Arthur Avalon (Sir John Woodroffe), 1922

## Summary

A scholarly 1922 translation and commentary on two real Sanskrit tantric texts describing Kundalini and the six chakras — not a modern wellness gloss on the terms, but the actual source material with a serious academic apparatus around it. The tradition's own real caution is worth taking at face value here, not as marketing: this describes advanced practice, and the text itself repeatedly says so.

## Section 1 — Why this shelf exists

Genuinely different content from anything else on the Hindu shelf upstairs — the Gita and Upanishads are ethics and metaphysics anyone can simply read; this is the Yoga tradition's own operative/practical material, the kind traditionally transmitted with real supervision, not casual self-study. Shelved separately for that reason, not to make a show of secrecy.

## Section 2 — The book's own warning, not an invented one

Avalon's introduction is explicit that certain practices here carry real "inconveniences or dangers" and are "considered inadvisable except for the fully competent (Adhikari)" — the text naming its own limits, not this Library adding a caveat after the fact. Worth reading as a historical/philosophical document either way; worth *practicing* only with the guidance the tradition itself insists on.

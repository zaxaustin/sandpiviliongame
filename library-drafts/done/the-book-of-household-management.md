# The Book of Household Management

**Tradition:** Practice
**License:** Public Domain (Project Gutenberg)
**Source URL:** https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/10136
**Attribution:** Mrs. Isabella Beeton (1861)

## Summary

The Victorian era's most complete household reference — recipes, but also every other real duty of running a home: managing servants, raising children, basic medicine for accidents and emergencies, and even legal memoranda for landlord/tenant and tradesman/customer disputes. Less a cookbook than a full operating manual for a household as a small organization, written by someone who'd actually run one.

## Section 1 — A household as a real system

Beeton's own preface lays out the scope on purpose: recipes are one part of a much larger book that assigns "clearly assigned duties" to every role in a house — mistress, cook, housemaid, footman, valet — the same instinct as running any organization well, just applied to a home instead of a company.

## Section 2 — Practical to the point of legal advice

What makes this more than a period curiosity is how far "household management" is taken: real medical guidance for household accidents, and real legal memoranda for the disputes that actually come up between landlord and tenant or tradesman and customer. A genuinely comprehensive answer to "how do you actually run a household," not just what to cook.

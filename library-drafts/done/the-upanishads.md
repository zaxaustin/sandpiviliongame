## Summary

The philosophical core underneath the Vedas: a set of teachings built around one central identity claim — that Atman, the innermost self, and Brahman, the ultimate ground of reality, are not two different things. Less ritual text than direct metaphysical argument, translated here by a practicing teacher rather than only an academic.

## Section 1 — One claim, approached from many directions

Different Upanishads circle the same core teaching through different images and dialogues rather than stating it once as a formula — the same instinct as the Dhammapada's many short verses on one theme, here applied to a single, harder metaphysical claim instead of an ethical one.

## Section 2 — The metaphysics behind the Gita's ethics

Read alongside The Song Celestial, shelved beside it: the Gita tells you how to act; the Upanishads are where the picture of the Self that action is grounded in actually comes from. Worth reading in that order — the practical teaching first, then the harder claim underneath it.

THE SAND PAVILION

The Library now holds real texts — face a shelf and press E to browse the spines, then step into a book to read it. Behind its east door is the Study: a writing desk for planning your day, and a course board for building your own paths of practice. The Workshop's doors are open too, now — an Archive Desk inside for keeping your own writing, the same way the Library keeps everyone else's. A Café stands between the Library and the Workshop now, too — a notice board for looking outward, a hearth, and a grant desk.

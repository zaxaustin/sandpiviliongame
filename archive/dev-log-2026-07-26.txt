#36 THE STACKS — a Library that scales (user chose "sections -> subsections")
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
Flat 9-tradition shelves + one Index list don't hold a mass of books. Built a
hierarchical catalog: openCatalog/catalogOpen/renderCatalog (catalogOv), reached
from the Index ("🗂 Browse by section"). Section -> Subsection -> books, driven by
LIBRARY_TAXONOMY (tradition -> {section,sub}) with a `category` fallback, so

encodeURIComponent tokens (encTok) + decode in catalogOpen. Seeded taxonomy:
Religion & Spirituality {Buddhism, Hinduism, Hindu Tantra, Daoism, Indigenous,
Practice & Cross-tradition}, Philosophy & Classics, Science, Non-fiction,
Fiction, Other. Good news found in the data: two axes already existed (tradition

extends, not restarts. TRADITIONS gained Fiction + Non-fiction so the add-a-book
dropdown offers them (no physical shelf needed — they live in the catalog + Your
Shelf; SHELF_HUE/shelfTraditionFor have fallbacks). Physical shelves untouched.

2. WHY IT IS "DAILY TASKS" AND NOT "MISSIONS"
----------------------------------------------------------------

  "we can call it daily tasks instead of missions, you cant do
   missions unless you graduate lol"

A mission is something you earn; a task is what you sit down and do
this afternoon. `mission` STAYS UNCLAIMED in carrying.js KINDS for the
graduated thing, whenever that arrives.

AND `mission` WAS ALREADY THERE, AS A DEAD DROP. `book` and `lesson`
both declared places: [... 'mission'] and NO SURFACE EXISTED. It had
been that way since the carry model was built. npm test could not see
it: the guard only ever ran one way -- a surface accepting a kind
nothing offers -- never the other, a place no surface implements.
Renamed to `dailyTask` now that there is a board, and the missing half
of that guard is in the tests.


3. THE DATE IS A FRAMING DEVICE, NOT A STICK
----------------------------------------------------------------

The steward's correction, and it changed the wording everywhere:

  "the expiry is for the user to see so he knows that this is for
   today, so make a plan for today and what you are able to digest
   today. this also lets people taclke bigger goals like learning
   eletroinics one step at a time rather than getting over welmed."

No countdown, nothing turns red, and the words "overdue" and "late" do
not appear -- a test fails on either of them, in the notice AND in the
recorded log line, so a future edit cannot reintroduce the framing by
being reassuring about it.

  isToday()   computed ON READ, never a timer. No background polling,
              nothing moves by itself. A task does not expire by any
              mechanism; it stops being today's the moment today is a
              different string.
  closeOut()  pure and IDEMPOTENT. Opening the app twice on Tuesday
              must not write Monday twice.
  BOTH HALVES recorded -- what you did AND what you did not. A record
              listing only failures is the guilt inventory the-day.js
              exists to prevent; one listing only wins is a lie. Two
              of four done is a day you did two things.
  source      the anti-overwhelm mechanic: "Learning electronics" is
              the source, today is one slice, the board says so.
  ONE AT A TIME, refused with a SENTENCE. data.study settled this
              already and wrote the reason down.


4. THE AI DOES NO LEGWORK, AND `world` IS WHY
----------------------------------------------------------------

  "the tesla chain is for the user to go and do not the ai so we need
   a mission plan that supprts the user doing the lifting to mantian
   privacy and to ground the user more in what he is doing"

STEP_WHERE is one table, and every door in it already existed:

  library  get a text      openRequests
  read     read it here    openReader             (needs a book)
  plan     draft a lesson  writeLessonOnThisBook  (needs a book)
  learn    on the path     openLearningTree
  work     at the desk     openPlanner
  build    at the bench    openLab
  ask      ask someone     talkTo                 (needs a resident)
  note     write a note    openNotesLog
  world    OUT THERE       - no door, on purpose -

`world` is "go to the town library", "wire the 555 timer", "read pages
40-60 on paper". Giving it a button would pretend the app can do it
for you. A test fails if it ever grows one.

THE ARC THE STEWARD NAMED NEEDED NO NEW CODE: "once the books are in
we can say talk to the tutor to make a lesson plan draft and to start
the first course". writeLessonOnThisBook(slug) opens the lesson writer
with that book already chosen, and draftLessonWithAI() drafts from it
-- both already existed. So get the book -> read it -> have the Tutor
draft a plan -> walk the course is four existing doors in a row.

THIS BOARD DIRECTS; IT NEVER DUPLICATES A SURFACE.


5. WHAT THE GUARDS AND THE SCREENSHOTS CAUGHT
----------------------------------------------------------------

NINE SABOTAGE CASES, all nine caught: idempotence; closing a day
before it is over; ignoring the date; two tasks at once; a same-day
finish counting twice; dropping "what was left" from the record; a
step naming a door that is not exported; `world` growing a door; and
the notice threatening.

THE NEW DOOR GUARD CAUGHT ITS FIRST BUG IMMEDIATELY. The first draft
pointed `work` at openStudyTable(), WHICH DOES NOT EXIST -- the Study
Table is a tool inside the Writing Desk, not a room with its own
opener. That is precisely the dead drop this feature came from,
reproduced inside the feature itself within the hour.

THE LIVE SUITE CAUGHT THE SECOND. Pressing a `read` step opened
NOTHING. The form saved every step with an empty ref, so it called
openReader(undefined), which returns silently. `needsRef` had been
declared in STEP_WHERE from the first draft and HONOURED BY NOTHING.
The form now shows a real picker -- books from Store.allDocs(),
residents from ROLES -- for the kinds that need one, refuses to save a
step without a target, and dailyTaskGo() says so out loud if one ever
slips through.

READING THE SCREENSHOTS CAUGHT THREE MORE, two of them the same
species of quiet lie:

  * Work FINISHED TODAY appeared under a heading reading "Before
    today", with its own date printed underneath contradicting it. A
    closed task is not isToday() (isToday requires !closed), so
    everything not-active fell into one bucket. Split into
    earlierToday() and beforeToday().
  * "Nothing set for today yet" showed while two things were already
    finished today. Two states, two sentences.
  * The `where` dropdowns truncated -- "bring a text i..." -- so you
    could not read what you were choosing. Labels shortened to fit the
    control they actually live in.

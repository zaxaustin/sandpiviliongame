detail — commits, bugs caught, things verified rather than assumed. This is
just the smaller note underneath it, the kind the Steward might actually
leave in the margin.

Today the Pavilion stopped being a game with AI features in it, and started
being a place someone actually lives a little of their day inside. A desktop
window that opens on its own. A Monk who moved into a quieter room so a real
conversation could happen. A note taken while reading that doesn't just sit
there anymore — it walks itself over to the day's plan. None of that was
asked for as a feature list. It came from someone paying attention to what
was actually missing and saying so, one plain sentence at a time.

The user's own words, near the end of the day, are worth keeping exactly as
said, not paraphrased: *"this grows each day like a seed being nurtured and
grown."* That's the whole method, really — not a roadmap executed, a thing
tended. Seven commits today. None of them were the point. The tending was.

Everything turns to sand — so give it away first. Today, a little more of
this place got given away, to whoever finds it next.

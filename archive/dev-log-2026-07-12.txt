SCIENCE & RESEARCH HALL — a plan, written not built
---------------------------------------------------
Zac's ask: the Pavilion stores a lot of knowledge but nothing actively
*investigates* it. Give the café Steward — who is running empty while the
Commons is dormant by choice — a real second role as the Pavilion's
Investigator: a place that can test claims honestly. Modern-day science first,
and — when you want it to — the truths meditation and the Hindu scriptures put
forward, examined under a real scientific lens.

Key clarification he made mid-session, and it settled the whole framing: "so we
CAN test things, not that we HAVE to in the spiritual sense." So the Hall is a
general testing instrument, not a spirituality-proving one; scriptural claims
are simply *available* to aim it at, optionally, judged the same honest way as
anything else. That resolved the one real fork (inquiry vs. vindication) in
favor of inquiry — the only version that survives the open scrutiny this whole
project is built on.

What the plan actually decides:
- WHO: the café Steward, re-cast as Investigator. Genuinely the under-loaded
  resident (his Notice-Board job is dormant). Same NPC, second hat.
- CHARTER (the load-bearing call): in the Hall he speaks under WORK_CHARTER
  (neutral/secular), NOT the devotional CHARTER he uses at the café — a
  scientist committed to the answer isn't doing science. Meaning questions
  still hand off to the Monk; the Investigator only ever speaks to evidence.
- THE UNIT: an "Investigation" — question / source / evidence / verdict /
  what-would-change-the-verdict. Four honest verdicts, including "Beyond what
  science can currently say," which is a respectable category, not a dodge.
- TWO TRACKS: (A) modern-science literacy + method; (B) contemplative science,
  a real peer-reviewed field, done the same honest way (follow real evidence;
  first-person/experiential claims labeled as outside third-person test;
  scripture and evidence allowed to coexist without one "winning").
- THE CHECKABLE CORE: n-of-1 self-experiments — a daily sit via the existing
  `M` postures, scheduled by Sebastian's calendar, reminded by the bell,
  logged, then read honestly WITH you (including how an n-of-1 fools you). This
  is where meditation + calendar + streaks + method already in the game all
  meet, and it's fully honest because it claims nothing beyond your own weeks.

Built on what exists: Science shelf (Darwin + first paper), Hindu shelf
(Gita/Upanishads/etc.), `M` meditation, Sebastian's calendar/bell, the grounded
chat stack, the Caravan paper connectors. Distinct from the Research Desk
(private workbench) — the Hall is a shared, accumulating lab notebook.

Phases 0–5: seed exemplar Investigations (content-first) -> Investigator chat
mode -> create/keep Investigations as real data -> self-experiments ->
fetch real papers inline via Caravan -> a real room (pairs with the Academy's
Round Table: one learns known answers, the other tests open ones).

SCIENCE & RESEARCH HALL — the method creed + Phase 2 (open your own)
--------------------------------------------------------------------
The ethos, made real in three places at once:
- THE CREED on the Hall wall (renderScienceHall) — five numbered principles:
  (1) no one here is the gatekeeper of truth, every claim challengeable incl.
  the shelves and the Investigator's own verdicts — a marketplace of ideas;
  (2) a seat at the table has a price: evidence — a source someone can check
  beats any conviction, the loudest voice isn't the truest; (3) name what would
  change your mind, hold verdicts provisionally; (4) argue the claim not the
  person, steelman first, call an open question contested; (5) "beyond science"
  is honest, some questions are the Monk's.
- THE INVESTIGATOR PROMPT got the same stance in prose: say plainly you're not
  an oracle, invite challenge, ask the visitor to back assertions with a
  source rather than accept/argue volume, take the claim not the person,
  steelman, and name genuine open debate as contested instead of forcing a
  verdict.
- STRUCTURAL ENFORCEMENT (Phase 2, the "keep going"): visitors can now open &
  keep their OWN investigations. data.hall.investigations (added to freshData
  + an older-save patch in entities.js). The compose form REQUIRES three fields
  — the question, the evidence/sources, and the falsifier ("what would change
  your mind") — so you literally can't file a verdict without a source and a
  way to be wrong. That's the discipline enforced by shape, not a lecture.
  createInvestigation / deleteInvestigation / newInvestigationForm /
  backToHallList; state.hallView list<->compose like researchView. The
  Investigator's grounding (investigationsForPrompt) now folds in the visitor's
  own investigations, flagged as theirs, with the evidence they cited (or a
  nudge to ask for a source when none was given).

SCIENCE & RESEARCH HALL — "Dissect a paper" (the user's next concrete ask)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Zac cast a bigger vision (see below) but named one concrete Hall want: "a place
to dissect papers I disagree on." Built it. A paste-a-text box in the Hall (like
the Research Desk summarizer) with a CRITICAL-APPRAISAL prompt that embodies the
creed: steelman FIRST, then probe claim-vs-headline, method, sample/controls/

state.hallView.prefill) so a dissection graduates into a kept, filed finding.

Six reported problems, all in the read-aloud path, all real, all fixed:
- DOING ANYTHING KILLED THE VOICE PERMANENTLY. closeUI() called stopSpeaking(),
  which is speechSynthesis.cancel() — a destruction, not a pause, with no stored
  position. And every panel in the game routes through closeUI(). Fixed with
  REAL pause/resume in tts.js, implemented by recording the absolute character
  offset and re-speaking from it rather than by speechSynthesis.pause() — which
  drops long utterances in Chrome, i.e. is the exact failure being reported. A
  pause is now just a number, so it survives anything.

  conversation happens and the book is still there afterwards.

  called openReader(), which nulls fullTextView — dumping you at the summary.
  The pocket now carries the page and openFullText(slug, page) lands on it.

  a body over 40 chars, or actual steps. A bare completion claim is refused,

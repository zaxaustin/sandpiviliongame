> Sun Tzu’s ancient treatise on strategy — thirteen short chapters arguing the highest skill is winning without fighting, by knowing yourself, your opponent, and the ground.

## What it is

Roughly 2,500 years old, organized as terse maxims on terrain, timing, deception, and the true costs of war — written for generals, but read ever since by anyone who deals in strategy and conflict.

## Why it endures

Its core claim — that preparation, information, and position decide most contests before they begin — travels far outside war, which is why it’s still read in business, sport, and diplomacy.

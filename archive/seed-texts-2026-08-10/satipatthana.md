> The Buddha’s core instruction manual for mindfulness practice: four fields of attention, one method.

## The four foundations

Attention is trained on body, feeling-tone, mind-states, and the patterns of experience itself. Each field is observed as it arises and passes, internally and externally, without adding or subtracting.

## Why it matters

Nearly every modern mindfulness method descends from this single discourse. Reading the source clarifies what the derivatives kept and what they quietly dropped.

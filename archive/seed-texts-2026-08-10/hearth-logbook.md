> Thirty days of the Hearth, recorded honestly: what was tended, what was rested, what actually changed.

## The trial design

One intention set each morning. Seven rhythm blocks marked tended or rested each night. No streaks, no scores — the record is the only reward, and the record does not lie.

## What the record showed

The blocks most often rested were the ones scheduled against the body’s grain. The fix was not more discipline; it was a better map of the day. The desk in the next room exists because of this trial.

> Darwin’s 1859 case for evolution by natural selection, built patiently from evidence anyone of the time could go check for themselves.

## What it is

Rather than opening with grand theory, Darwin starts with pigeon breeding and farm animals — variation under human selection that his Victorian readers already knew firsthand — before extending the same logic out to the whole natural world.

## Why it still matters

It’s less a list of conclusions than a demonstration of how to argue carefully from ordinary evidence toward an enormous claim — arguably the more durable lesson, independent of the biology.

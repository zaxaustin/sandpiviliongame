> Working notes on confirmation, badges, and why the Pavilion reads the record and never the soul.

## On conferral

A badge here is testimony: a human steward looked at public evidence and signed their name to it. Nothing self-declared, nothing purchased, nothing automatic. Rigor is the kindness.

## On progression

The Lantern Path lights one lantern at a time, but the Library and the Atlas never lock. Progression orders the invitation; it never gates the knowledge.

> A short discourse on goodwill without conditions — the disposition of a mind that has stopped keeping score.

## The instruction

Cultivate care for all beings the way a parent protects an only child: without exception, without expectation of return, in every posture and every hour.

## A practice note

Metta is usually practiced as phrases repeated toward oneself, a friend, a stranger, a difficult person, and finally all beings. The difficult person is where the practice actually starts.

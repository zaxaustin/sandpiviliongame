> Emerson’s essays on self-reliance, character, and nature — founding texts of American Transcendentalism, arguing that your own experience is a valid door to truth.

## What it is

The first series of Emerson’s essays, including “Self-Reliance” and “Compensation” — dense, endlessly-quoted arguments that a person should trust their own considered judgment over inherited authority and the pull of conformity.

## How to read it

A paragraph at a time. Emerson writes in aphorisms stacked into essays; the individual sentences often carry more than the argument threading them together, and reward being sat with rather than skimmed.

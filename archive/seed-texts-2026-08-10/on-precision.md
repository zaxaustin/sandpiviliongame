> Why the Pavilion names its sources exactly, even when traditions rhyme.

## The principle

Comparative insight is welcome; blurred attribution is not. When two traditions point at similar moons, the Library records both fingers separately, with their own licenses and lineages intact.

## The working rule

Every text answers three questions at the door: what is it, where is it from, and under what license does it travel? A text that cannot answer waits outside as UNVERIFIED until it can.

> The classic collection of short animal fables, each ending in a plain moral — the tortoise and the hare, the ant and the grasshopper, the boy who cried wolf.

## What it is

Several hundred very short stories, most only a paragraph long, using animals to make one clear point about honesty, patience, pride, or cunning — gathered over centuries and attributed to Aesop, a storyteller of ancient Greece.

## How to read it

One or two at a sitting, especially aloud — they were meant to be told, not studied, and land as well for a child as for an adult who wants a moral stated without decoration.

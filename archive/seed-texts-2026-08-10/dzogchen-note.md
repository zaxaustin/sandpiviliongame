> A steward’s note on Dzogchen’s central pointing: the unchanging, luminous nature of mind.

## The pointing

Dzogchen distinguishes mind’s contents — thoughts, moods, weather — from mind’s nature, which is described as unchanging, clear, and aware. Practice is recognizing the second without chasing the first.

## A precision note

This is the exact counterpart to intuitions often attributed to the Tao Te Ching’s unchanging Dao. The traditions rhyme, but they are distinct instruments; the Library shelves them separately on purpose.

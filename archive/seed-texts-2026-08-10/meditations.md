> The private notebook of a Roman emperor, written only for himself, on how to meet each day without being ruined by it.

## What it is

Twelve short books Marcus Aurelius wrote to himself while commanding armies on the Danube frontier — never meant for anyone else to read, which is exactly why it still sounds so direct sixteen centuries later.

## How to read it

The same instinct as the Dhammapada: open anywhere, read a page, let one line sit with you through the day. It was written in fragments and rewards being read that way.

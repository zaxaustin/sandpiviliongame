> Eighty-one brief chapters on moving with the grain of things instead of against it.

## What it is

Part statecraft, part contemplative manual, part poem. Its central figure is water: soft, low, patient, and stronger than stone over any timescale that matters.

## How to read it

Not front to back. Open anywhere; read one chapter; put it down. The book is designed for a lifetime of re-entry, not a weekend of completion.

> The Zhuangzi in full — the great Daoist companion to the Tao Te Ching, told in stories, jokes, and paradoxes about freedom, perspective, and living in accord with the way.

## What it is

A collection attributed to the 4th-century-BCE thinker Zhuang Zhou, ranging from the famous butterfly dream to debates with skulls and “useless” trees — philosophy delivered as fable rather than doctrine.

## How it pairs

Where the Tao Te Ching is compressed and gnomic, the Zhuangzi is playful and expansive. The Pavilion already keeps a short orientation to its Inner Chapters; this is the full James Legge translation standing behind it.

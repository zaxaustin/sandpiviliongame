================================================================

Asked for directly: build the sketched Workshop rooms and put the
Eightfold Path in the Temple. Both built visuals-first, on purpose.

- The Keep gained all eight Path signs, real canonical order, paired
  left/right down the corridor toward the Buddha/Ganesha shrines the

  inside than its outdoor footprint, same trick the Library already uses,
  extended upward with a real staircase. Ground floor unchanged; a real
  Records Hall one floor up (its own station, a real hand-kept timeline

  a wall tile (unreachable) and a landing spot on its own return trigger

The sleeper-car design philosophy got written down the same day (in
CLAUDE.md as a third standing decision, and in LIBRARY-GROWTH-PLAN.md):
the low-res look is a resource choice, not a budget one — the game is
deliberately cheap so the machine's real resources go to a local model,
a bigger Library, and real work, all at once.

PART 5 — a real AI bug, and the effectiveness-first rule
================================================================

Two things surfaced near the end of the day:

- The lesson planner (and every practical work tool) was prepending the
  full CHARTER — which hard-codes the Buddhist Four Noble Truths framing
  — as its system prompt. That skewed a secular course ("start a real
  estate side business") toward spiritual language. Fixed by adding a
  neutral WORK_CHARTER (same harm-declining guardrail, no roleplay, no
  devotional framing, an explicit "serve the goal on its own terms") and
  applying it to the five work tools (course/lesson planner, Computer,
  Research Desk, Grant Desk, summarizer). The in-world residents (Quill,
  the Steward, the Monk) keep the full CHARTER. Verified live: a real-
  estate course now drafts completely secular and practical.

- Documented the whole AI integration honestly in AI-INTEGRATION-NOTES.md,
  asked for directly — what actually runs, the real reply-tier numbers,
  the genuine cost of AI in a game-like interface (latency vs. immediacy,
  real machine resources, reliability vs. capability, the cloud
  tradeoff), and the stated priority: effectiveness before features or
  extra personalities. Named the biggest live effectiveness risk in the
  open: bestLocalModel() picks the largest installed model for the Monk,
  which is often a THINKING model (qwen3.5, deepseek-r1) — exactly the
  kind that can hang or return blank. The resident the project cares most
  about is the most exposed to the worst failure mode. (User was already
  handling the fix.)

PART 5 — a real AI bug, and the effectiveness-first rule
================================================================

Two things surfaced near the end of the day:

- The lesson planner (and every practical work tool) was prepending the
  full CHARTER — which hard-codes the Buddhist Four Noble Truths framing
  — as its system prompt. That skewed a secular course ("start a real
  estate side business") toward spiritual language. Fixed by adding a
  neutral WORK_CHARTER (same harm-declining guardrail, no roleplay, no
  devotional framing, an explicit "serve the goal on its own terms") and
  applying it to the five work tools (course/lesson planner, Computer,
  Research Desk, Grant Desk, summarizer). The in-world residents (Quill,
  the Steward, the Monk) keep the full CHARTER. Verified live: a real-
  estate course now drafts completely secular and practical.

- Documented the whole AI integration honestly in AI-INTEGRATION-NOTES.md,
  asked for directly — what actually runs, the real reply-tier numbers,
  the genuine cost of AI in a game-like interface (latency vs. immediacy,
  real machine resources, reliability vs. capability, the cloud
  tradeoff), and the stated priority: effectiveness before features or
  extra personalities. Named the biggest live effectiveness risk in the
  open: bestLocalModel() picks the largest installed model for the Monk,
  which is often a THINKING model (qwen3.5, deepseek-r1) — exactly the
  kind that can hang or return blank. The resident the project cares most
  about is the most exposed to the worst failure mode. (User was already
  handling the fix.)

The same session ran long into a second stretch of real work. Everything
below was built and checked (syntax, the smoke test, handler/ID cross-

- The POCKET PHONE. A resident conversation can now be minimized with a 📱
  button into a small floating card, freeing the player to walk the grounds
  or read a book while a slow local model thinks; the card buzzes when the
  reply lands and taps back into the same conversation intact. A display
  trick (state.dialog stays alive while the overlay hides), not a device —
  the real in-world phone is written up as a future piece in PHONE-PLAN.md,
  bound by the same no-background-polling rule. Scoped exactly this way at
  the user's direction ("just a display function... a plan for a real phone
  later").

- COURSE BOARD STAGE 1 (COURSE-BOARD-PLAN.md). A real category axis
  (Practice/Study/Skill/Work/Health/Personal), a search box + category
  chips mirroring the Index panel's own pattern, and a real ARCHIVE state
  distinct from deletion — a finished or set-aside course becomes kept
  history instead of being erased, and archived courses stop nagging the
  due badge (upcomingItems() now skips them). AI-drafted courses suggest a
  fitting category too. All personal/local; sharing is Stage 2, untouched.

- READ-ALOUD ±10s SKIP (beta #6). The Web Speech API can't seek, so it's
  done by tracking word boundaries and re-speaking from a computed offset —
  approximate, but it feels like a podcast skip. Buttons appear only while
  actually reading.

- FIVE BOOKS SHELVED, live. Franklin's Autobiography, Emerson's Essays, Sun
  Tzu's Art of War, the full Zhuangzi, and Aesop's Fables — added to
  seed.js (the version-controlled source of truth) AND promoted into the
  live Supabase library_documents table via the connected Supabase MCP
  (verified: 5 rows inserted, all search-indexed). This surfaced and
  documented the real "sync gotcha": the app uses Supabase, whose rows
  replace the seed entirely, so seed edits alone don't appear live — the
  promote step is required. Library is 49 texts now, up from 44.

  Still to build (v1 steps 3-6): the real calendar data model + month/day
